# Third-party projects

808SGN Stem Lab does not bundle `audio-separator`, model weights or their
source code. The installer retrieves dependencies from their normal package
sources.

- [python-audio-separator](https://github.com/nomadkaraoke/python-audio-separator)
  — MIT-licensed separation engine maintained by Nomad Karaoke and
  contributors.
- [Ultimate Vocal Remover](https://github.com/Anjok07/ultimatevocalremovergui)
  — model ecosystem and work by Anjok07, aufr33 and other model authors.
- [Demucs](https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs)
  — music source-separation architecture.

Users are responsible for checking the terms associated with any downloaded
model weights and for having permission to process their input audio.
