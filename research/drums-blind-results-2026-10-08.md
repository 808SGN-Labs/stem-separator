# StemLab — Drums blind listening results
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: three controls completed; one listener; descriptive pilot evidence.

Five scored criteria: drum isolation (melodic/vocal/non-drum bleed), snare integrity (stored key `transient_integrity`), cymbal integrity, kick integrity, remix usability. Older `groove_integrity` values are preserved in the original scores but excluded from the five-criterion analysis.

All 60 current-criteria ratings complete. Mapping was used only after completion; candidate letters and secret mappings are intentionally omitted.

Balanced/htdemucs has the highest mean for drums (4.33/5), but model rankings differ by source control. This is a small single-listener pilot, not a statistical quality guarantee.
