## Naming rules

- Write `808SGN` and `MTM DSP` in uppercase.
- Use `808sgn-labs` only for handles and URLs.
- Use `MTM DSP` in public credits and citations.
- Keep legal-name and private-account information out of repository content,
  Git metadata, screenshots and example paths.
- Do not introduce additional aliases as product publishers. Historical names
  belong in a separate biography, not repository metadata.
